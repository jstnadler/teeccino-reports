PDP Image Lightening — Review

116 candidates out of 575 live PDP carousel images across 90 products · generated 2026-08-06 by AssetSmith
Read this before approving in bulk.These are candidates ranked by how much tonal headroom each image has - not confirmed defects. A dark bag is not the same thing as a dark image, so nothing here is called wrong until you look at it. The other 459 live PDP images already have healthy highlights and are not shown.

The correction is a white-point lift: the image is scaled so its brightest 1% reaches near-white, with a soft roll-off so nothing clips and the transparent background is untouched. On the one case where the right answer is known - Cacao + 5, corrected by Brendon by hand - this method landed within 3.9 luminance of his file.

Only the 71 "new packaging" ones are likely worth doing. The 8 tagged old packaging get replaced by the rollover anyway, and the 37 tagged brand graphic are full-bleed marketing panels where lifting the white point shifts the brand background colour. Both are shown for completeness but recommended as keep-as-is.

Look at the clustering before deciding one by one. The entire mushroom tea line and most Mediterranean teas sit at almost exactly the same +16%. That looks like a characteristic of the render batch, not 116 independent mistakes. If Brendon is happy with how that batch looks, deny that group wholesale - the genuinely unusual ones are at the top.
