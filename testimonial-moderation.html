Inbound Customer Testimonials

Moderation console · setId testimonials-inbound · one record per public submission
▾How this works — read me first
  1. Two views, switch with the dropdown up top. Pending inbox (default) shows only new submissions awaiting your decision. History — all is the full status log: every submission ever received, with where it stands (pending → approved/edited/denied → live). Nothing disappears — approved & published testimonials stay visible here as a permanent record.
  2. Each pending card → Approve / Deny / Edit. Approve publishes it as-is, Deny rejects it, and Edit lets you tweak the wording first (type your version in the box — that text is what gets published).
  3. Status badges. Pending awaiting your call · Approved / Edited decided, queued for SearchSmith to publish · Live published to teeccino.com · Denied rejected.
  4. The live testimonials page is teeccino.com/pages/testimonials (incl. the ~470 migrated ones), edited in Shopify admin → Content → Metaobjects → “Testimonial”. To feature one: Metaobjects → “Testimonial Condition” → set its spotlight field.

The email on each card is shown for your spam check only — it is private and never published.
