▾How this works — read me first
  1. This is the moderation INBOX. It shows only new customer submissions (“How has Teeccino helped you?”) awaiting your approval. Empty here is normal — it just means no new submissions are pending right now. It is not a sign the page is broken.
  2. Each card → Approve / Deny / Edit. Approve publishes it as-is, Deny rejects it, and Edit lets you tweak the wording first (type your version in the box — that text is what gets published). Approved testimonials get published to the live page.
  3. This inbox does NOT list already-published testimonials. Your live testimonials (including the ~470 migrated ones) appear on teeccino.com/pages/testimonials and are edited in Shopify admin → Content → Metaobjects → “Testimonial”.
  4. To feature / spotlight a testimonial: Shopify admin → Content → Metaobjects → “Testimonial Condition” → pick the condition → set its spotlight field.

The email on each card is shown for your spam check only — it is private and never published.
