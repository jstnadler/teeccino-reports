This panel is measured on ship date, not order date — and on a wider set of orders than the panels above it. The warehouse ships on business days, so this window contains 18 weekend days and 1 weekday(s) with no shipments (holiday or shutdown). Per Shipping Day (150) divides by the 41 days that actually shipped and is the real throughput rate. Per Calendar Day (103) divides the same orders by all 60 days and so understates output by 32% — it is shown only so the two are not mistaken for each other. Shipment data runs through 2026-09-08.
Counts 6,152 orders — DTC, Target and Walmart — which is 97.9% of the 6,281 orders that shipped in this window. The other 129: 128 non-Shopify shipments (SO… and WW… order numbers from other systems, with no Shopify order to match) and 1 re-shipment of orders whose first shipment predates this window. The non-Shopify shipments are real warehouse work this panel does not yet count; they are named rather than absorbed so the shortfall stays visible. An order is counted once, on its first shipment date.
