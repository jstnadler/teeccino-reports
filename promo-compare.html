Aligned by day of promo, not calendar date — day 1 against day 1 — so promotions of different lengths can be read against each other. Hover any chart for that day's figures and its real date.
Totals are what the window actually did; per-day averages are what makes windows of different lengths comparable. A longer sale wins every total by construction.
Gross sales is before discounts; net revenue is after. The promo hub's own tile quotes the NET figure, so the two pages will show different sales numbers for the same sale and both are correct — e.g. Labor Day through Sep 7 is $56,554 gross / $49,064 net on 854 orders. Gross sales and orders are direct-to-consumer. Ad spend is Google plus Meta. ROAS here is gross sales divided by ad spend on the same days — it is not an attribution model and does not claim the ads caused the sales.
