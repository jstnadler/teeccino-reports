📊 Weekly production efficiency — planned vs actual

For each week: how many labor-hours the runs should have taken (expected hrs = the planned rate × packs actually made) versus how many they actually took (actual hrs = run time × operators). Efficiency = expected ÷ actual — 100% means we hit plan; under 100% means a run took longer than planned. The gap is priced at the confirmed $26.06/hr wage → the overrun $ is real labor money. Finished-goods lines only (blending is its own stage); labor only, no machine overhead. A ⚠ in a week's detail means that SKU's planned rate looks wrong vs its own history — fix the plan, don't blame the line. Click a week to drill in.
