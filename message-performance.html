Read this first. ROAS on this page is platform-attributed — Meta's and Google's own reported conversion value divided by spend. It is not Shopify truth and it is not incremental. Use it to rank messages against each other, never as a profitability number. Prospecting and retargeting are never pooled: retargeting ROAS runs 4–7× against prospecting's 0.5–1.0×, so a blended figure just ranks messages by how much retargeting they happened to receive. Any figure below $1,000 of spend inside one funnel is withheld as thin.

The verdict

one card per primary message · ranked by prospecting CTR, not by spend
Spend is the input; attention is the outcome — so the cards are ordered by how well each message earned a click from a cold audience. Click any card to open its sub-messages and the individual creative concepts inside it. Prospecting shows impressions and reach; reach is the honest denominator for how many distinct people heard the message.

Google — campaign level

no creative read · no funnel split · never summed with Meta
Google is largely a product and intent channel, not a message channel. Campaign names carry a theme, but there is no per-ad creative data and PMax campaigns have no reliable funnel label — so there is no TOF/retargeting split here and these rows cannot be compared like-for-like with the Meta cards.
