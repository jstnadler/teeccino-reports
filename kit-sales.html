How this is counted

  • A kit is one set of bags added together from a kit page (they share a hidden kit id). Kit bag revenue is what customers paid for those bags after discounts, excluding add-ons, shipping and tax.
  • Subscribe kits have every bag on a subscription; One-time kits have none. A kit mixing both is counted as mixed.
  • Attach rate = share of kits bought with that add-on (French press, or 31015L (Prebiotic Creamer)).
  • Landing sessions are Shopify's own visit counts for visits that started on the kit page. A kit buyer may have arrived on another page first, so sessions and kits are shown side by side, not as a conversion rate.
Not kit-page sales (reference only): 267 orders since 2026-11-10 received the sitewide "Kit Savings" or "Kit Add-on: Free Shipping" automatic discount without any bag from a kit page. Those discounts apply to any cart with 2+ of the six kit bags, so they are left out of every figure above.
