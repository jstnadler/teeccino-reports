Advertised is the approved customer-facing percentage for each promotion — it is the number to use in creative. Functional is the internal discount setting that produces it; it is shown so the two are never confused, and it is not a number to publish. First-subscription functional values are an increment on Teeccino's standing subscription discount, not a discount off list price.
