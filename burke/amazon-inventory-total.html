Things to know about this number:
1. Weeks before 2026-07-13 are reconstructed from Amazon's FBA inventory ledger, not the live snapshot feed, and inbound for those weeks is an estimate (Amazon's ledger report has no inbound-shipment column) — modeled at the average inbound share measured across live snapshot days. Same approach, same caveats, as the Inventory Coverage dashboard, which also breaks this down against units sold (weeks of cover) if that's the view you want instead of a flat total.
2. January 2025 is the hard floor — that's the limit of what Amazon's ledger report returns.
3. The week of Oct 13–19, 2025 shows a sharp one-week dip (71,851 units, down from ~96k the week before, back to ~110k the next) — checked directly against the ledger and it's a real reading, not a computation error on this end, but it's a single-week swing worth treating with a little skepticism.
4. The current week is in progress (partial, shown as a gold dot) — it will keep updating until the week closes.
