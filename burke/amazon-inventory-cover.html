Two things to know about this number:
1. "Reserved" isn't purely FC transfer. Amazon's inventory feed we ingest gives one combined "reserved" bucket — FC transfer, FC processing, and customer orders together — not split out. So this ratio is the closest available reading of "inbound + FC transfer + available," not an exact isolation of FC transfer alone. Flagged to Justin/DataOps if you want the split broken out.
2. History starts 2026-07-13 — that's when DataOps' FBA inventory snapshot began. You're seeing every week we have (currently 4 complete + this week in progress); the trend will keep filling in from here. The in-progress week is shown separately and excluded from the trend line since only a few days of sales have posted.
