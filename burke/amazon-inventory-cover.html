Things to know about this number:
1. "Reserved" isn't purely FC transfer. Amazon's live inventory feed we ingest (2026-07-13 onward) gives one combined "reserved" bucket — FC transfer, FC processing, and customer orders together — not split out. So this ratio is the closest available reading of "inbound + FC transfer + available," not an exact isolation of FC transfer alone. Flagged to Justin/DataOps if you want the split broken out.
2. Weeks before 2026-07-13 are reconstructed from Amazon's FBA inventory ledger (DataOps backfill, 2025-01-01 onward), not the live snapshot feed. The ledger has no "available vs. reserved" split either, so those weeks show one combined figure under "Available."
3. Inbound before 2026-07-13 is an ESTIMATE, not a measurement. Amazon's ledger report carries no inbound-shipment column at all. We apply the average inbound share we measure in the live data (— across every day we have) to the ledger's inventory total for each older week. That ratio has swung as wide as 1.8%–25.5% day to day, so treat hatched/dashed values as directional, not exact — they're marked estimated: true in the underlying data. Flagged as a modeling choice, not a data fact.
4. January 2025 is the hard floor. That's the limit of what Amazon's ledger report will return; there's no way to go back further.
