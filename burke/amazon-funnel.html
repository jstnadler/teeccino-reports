The Buy Box problem is one dated event, not many ASIN problems.

Buy Box suppression event dated 2026-05-20: 22 ASINs, $12,978/mo in attributable gross. The cause is Amazon-side suppression, not competition — stockouts, our own pricing, a 3P seller flood, variation re-parenting and DTC undercutting were each ruled out with a clean negative. Needs a Seller Central check.

Basis: Dated off the Sponsored Products ad-delivery cliff (SP cannot serve without the featured offer): offenders serving ads fell 14→4 on 2026-05-20 while every campaign stayed ENABLED and account spend held flat. Dollars are a difference-in-differences read, offenders vs all other ASINs, Feb-Apr vs May-Jul.
