Social Media Audit

Prepared by McCain Kennedy, Set Fire Creative (agency pitch). Covers Instagram, Facebook, TikTok, Pinterest and LinkedIn.

Overall verdict

  • "Good foundation, underleveraged."

Posting cadence (posts per week, current → recommended)

  • Facebook: 4–5 → 5–6
  • Instagram: 2–3 → 5–6
  • TikTok: 6–7, with gaps → 5–6
  • Pinterest: unknown → 2–3
  • LinkedIn: 0 → 2–3

Their six recommended opportunities

  1. Shift from product-first to people-first content
  2. More customer stories and user-generated content
  3. More lifestyle and relatable content
  4. Make caffeine reduction a major content pillar
  5. Treat community management as part of the strategy
  6. Build recurring weekly social series
