What Stay.ai can cost before leaving pays — and where to go. Generated 2026-07-31 13:35. Model window: T12M to 2026-07-30.

Verdict — 0 of 32 cells make building rational

Building a subscription platform from scratch is never rational at any Stay.ai price in this matrix, because the relevant comparison is not Stay.ai — it is the free alternative. Shopify Subscriptions is $0 capex and $0 license, and the contracts, the payment vault, and the billing calendar already live in Shopify (Stay.ai only supplies the layer above). A from-scratch build carries $20,000–$40,000/yr of opex forever — so against the free option its payback is negative in every cell, at every fee, at every rev-share, on either base, before and after 20% growth. Even measured only against Stay.ai (ignoring the free option entirely), the best case in the whole matrix is 4.9 years, and just 0 of 32 cells clear a 3-year bar.

The live decision is therefore Stay.ai vs the free swap, worth $3,588–$32,211/yr today. That decision is not blocked on price — it is blocked on the Shopify support ticket confirming contract re-parenting.

5 · Assumptions, method & what this does not price
Inputs (confirmed by Justin 2026-07-31). T12M subscription revenue $674,104 · merch subtotal $661,187 · collected total $674,104 (incl. $42,500 shipping, net of $29,400 discounts) · ~2,200–2,500 active contracts · sub AOV $44.26.
Independent DB cross-check. calc_daily_shopify over the T12M window ending 2026-07-30: sub revenue $675,464 (+0.2% vs confirmed), 15,255 sub orders, AOV $44.28 (confirmed $44.26), 13,818 recurring + 1,437 first-time. The confirmed inputs hold — the model uses them, not the DB figures, so the two stay independent.
Formula. Stay.ai annual = fee × 12 + rev-share × base. payback = capex ÷ (alternative-avoided-cost − new opex); where that denominator is ≤ 0 there is no payback and the cell reads "never".
Build alternative. $60,000–$120,000 capex, $20,000–$40,000/yr opex. Opex is treated as perpetual — a subscription biller is not a build-once asset (PCI scope, dunning, card-updater, tax, plan migrations, on-call for a recurring-billing system against $674,104/yr).
Shopify Subscriptions alternative. $0 capex licence, $0/yr licence, per the brief. Shopify already owns the contracts, the payment vault, the billing-cycle calendar and checkout; Stay.ai supplies only the layer above — which is why the swap is a layer swap, not a rebuild.
What this model does NOT price. (1) Migration effort — theme cleanup across 18 legacy templates, the account-portal link, and a global.js null-guard; real but one-off and small. (2) Churn risk during cutover — the exit plan's risk register puts 5% churn at ≈$30K in year one and $50–90K in LTV, which is the single largest number in this whole analysis and dwarfs every licence-fee cell; it argues for a canary batch, not for staying. (3) Feature loss — Stay.ai's churn flows, swap UX and pause reasons have no direct Shopify Subscriptions equivalent; if those drive retention their value is a real offset that this licence-only model omits. (4) Shopify payment/transaction fees — unchanged by the choice, so excluded on both sides. (5) Contract re-parenting feasibility — the whole swap is blocked on a Shopify support ticket; if re-parenting and payment-method reuse are both impossible, the swap is off the table and this becomes a straight Stay.ai renegotiation.
How to read the verdict. "Rational to build" is judged against the best available alternative, which is the free one. Judging a build against Stay.ai alone is the classic error — it flatters the build by pretending the free option does not exist.
