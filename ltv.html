How to read this. Each cohort is the set of customers whose first-ever Teeccino order landed in that month (DTC only; wholesale excluded). Once acquired, a customer stays in their cohort forever — these figures are cumulative through 2026-06-25 and refresh nightly, so an older month keeps growing as those customers reorder. Repeat Rate = share with 2+ lifetime orders (subscription rebills count). Net Revenue = subtotal − discounts (excludes shipping & tax). Contribution Margin = Net − COGS − estimated shipping cost − card processing (2.42% + $0.33/order); it excludes ad spend, shown separately as acquisition CAC. Ad Spend = total Meta + Google spend in the acquisition month (the CAC numerator); CAC = Ad Spend ÷ new customers. Watch this for context: in early 2023 paid spend was minimal (Meta didn't start until Feb 2023), so those months show a very low CAC and an unusually high LTV:CAC that doesn't compare to later, fully-paid months. LTV:CAC = CM per customer ÷ that month's CAC (≥3x 1–3x <1x). Click a cohort month to open its MTD page. Use the toggle to split by first-order type: Blended = all new customers, One-time = first order was not a subscription, Subscription = first order was a subscription. New Cust, repeat rate, LTV and totals are segment-specific; Ad Spend and CAC stay cohort-level (paid spend can't be cleanly split by first-order type), so a segment's LTV:CAC reads its own CM/cust against the blended acquisition cost. Sub-First % = share of that cohort whose first order was a subscription (purple = subscription, track = one-time); it's a cohort property, so it stays the same across the segment toggle. Caveats: guest checkouts (no customer ID) excluded; shipping cost is the actual ShipStation cost mapped per order (pre-2024 orders with no match use a $$7.50 fallback); CAC spreads the acquisition month's ad spend across that month's new customers (cohort-level).

📊 Projected 2-Year LTV — Recent Cohorts
How this works: For each cohort that hasn't reached 24 months yet, we take its actual cumulative LTV at its current age, divide by the % of 2-yr LTV historically captured by that age (the "velocity curve" from complete cohorts), and scale to month 24.
Example: a cohort at 12 months with $90 actual LTV — if complete cohorts captured 73% of their 2-yr value by month 12 — projects to $90 / 0.73 = $123.
Caveats: assumes recent cohorts will retain like historical ones. If product mix or subscriber rate has shifted materially, projections drift. Cohorts younger than 6 months are flagged "very low confidence" — small denominator, high variance.

💰 Contribution Margin LTV vs CAC — Are These Cohorts Actually Profitable?
The real profitability question. Gross LTV (revenue per customer) is just an input; what matters is contribution margin per customer compared to cost to acquire them.

Contribution Margin LTV = Gross LTV − COGS − Shipping − Payment Processing.
  • COGS: SUM(line items × cogs_per_unit) per order — ~99% revenue coverage from the COGS table
  • Shipping: actual ShipStation cost per order when matched (2024+); $7.50 fallback for pre-2024 orders
  • Processing: 2.42% × revenue + $0.33/txn (fitted from actual Shopify Payments fees, ~3.06% all-in)

CAC = (Meta + Google ad spend in cohort acquisition month) ÷ (# new DTC customers acquired in that month). Treats all paid spend as acquisition cost — slightly overstates CAC since some spend goes to retargeting.

Profitable cohort: CM-LTV / CAC ≥ 1.0x means the cohort's 2-year contribution margin covers what we paid to acquire them. Industry rule of thumb: ≥3x is healthy.
What's NOT modeled: returns/refunds, organic costs (Klaviyo/Attentive subscriptions), warehousing, support, taxes. So actual cohort profit is somewhat lower than CM-LTV.
Reconciliation with the cohort grid above: both use the same cohorts (true first-order customers, Shopify lifetime order #1), so customer counts match exactly. Any small per-cohort gap is the shipping-cost estimate only — this section uses per-order matched shipping (with a fallback), while the grid uses a flat carrier-average per order.

🔁 Windowed Repurchase Rate — % of cohort placing a 2nd order within N days
Repurchase rate at day N = % of customers in a cohort who placed a 2nd order within N days of their 1st order. Subscription auto-ships count just like manual reorders.
This is the conventional ecommerce operational repurchase metric — complements the lifetime "Repeat rate" in the headline (which is ≥2 orders within 24 months).
Cohort eligibility: a cohort contributes to a given window only if that window has fully elapsed since cohort end-of-month. So the 30-day blend uses every cohort since 2023-01, but the 730-day blend uses only complete cohorts.
