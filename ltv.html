👥 2-Year Customer LTV

DTC cohort analysis — net revenue per acquired customer over 24 months. Generated 2026-07-16 07:51.

📖 Metric Index — every metric on this page, defined

Revenue & profit

Net revenue
subtotal − discounts (excludes shipping & tax). The realized sales value the customer paid for product.
COGS
Product cost: Σ(line qty × cost/unit) per order, from the COGS table (~99% coverage; scaled to 100% when partial).
Shipping cost
Actual per-order ShipStation carrier cost for 2024-onward orders (~100% matched by order number). 2023 orders predate ShipStation, so they use a $7.50 flat estimate — within ~2% of the $7.67 carrier average. So shipping is precise for recent cohorts, a calibrated estimate for the 2023 tail.
Gross profit
Net − COGS (before shipping). The "Profit basis" toggle switches the profit columns to this.
Contribution margin (CM)
Net − COGS − shipping cost — the same lean definition as the Monthly KPI dashboard. Ad spend is not subtracted here (it's the separate CAC). Card processing and the shipping-revenue credit are excluded to match the KPI walk. CM − CAC = the after-ad figure the KPI shows.
Card processing
Shopify Payments fees (~2.42% + $0.33/order). Tracked elsewhere; excluded from this page's CM so it matches the KPI.

Customers & cohorts

Cohort
The month of a customer's first-ever DTC order. They stay in that cohort forever; rows are cumulative through the as-of date. Wholesale excluded.
New customer
First-ever Teeccino order (Shopify lifetime order index = 1). Guest checkouts (no customer ID) and wholesale excluded.
Sub-First %
Share of the cohort whose first order was a subscription. A cohort property — constant across the segment toggle.
Subscriber vs one-time
Split by whether the customer's first order was a subscription (not whether they ever subscribed).
Orders / customer
Lifetime orders ÷ customers in the cohort (subscription rebills count as orders).

LTV & retention

2-year (24-mo) LTV
Cumulative net revenue per customer within 730 days of their first order.
Complete vs partial cohort
Complete = ≥730 days elapsed since the cohort's month-end (only these show a real 2-yr number). Partial values are marked value*.
Projected 2-yr LTV
For partial cohorts: actual LTV at current age ÷ the % of 2-yr value historically captured by that age (the velocity curve). Scales a young cohort to a 2-year estimate.
Velocity curve
% of the eventual 2-yr LTV that complete cohorts had already captured by month K. Drives the projection above.
Repeat rate / Loyal rate
Share of the cohort with ≥2 lifetime orders (repeat) / ≥3 (loyal). Rebills count.
Windowed repurchase rate
Share placing a 2nd order within N days of the first — an apples-to-apples retention read across cohorts of different ages.

Acquisition

CAC
Cost to acquire a customer = (Meta + Google spend in the acquisition month) ÷ new customers that month. Cohort-level — paid spend can't be cleanly split by first-order type.
LTV:CAC
Profit per customer (CM or Gross per the toggle) ÷ CAC. ≥3× healthy, 1–3× watch, <1× unprofitable.
Monthly revenue
The whole month's total DTC net revenue (all orders, new + returning) — NOT cohort-lifetime revenue. Pair with Ad Spend for that month's TACOS (Ad Spend ÷ Monthly Rev).
Channel
First-touch acquisition source (order_journey.first_visit_source) on the customer's first order, normalized.

How to read this. Each cohort is the set of customers whose first-ever Teeccino order landed in that month (DTC only; wholesale excluded). Once acquired, a customer stays in their cohort forever — these figures are cumulative through 2026-07-16 and refresh nightly, so an older month keeps growing as those customers reorder. Repeat Rate = share with 2+ lifetime orders (subscription rebills count). Net Revenue = subtotal − discounts (excludes shipping & tax). Contribution Margin = Net − COGS − shipping cost — the same lean definition as the Monthly KPI dashboard. Ad spend is not subtracted here; it's shown separately as acquisition CAC (the KPI folds ads into CM, but for a cohort that's the CAC, so we keep them apart and compare via LTV:CAC). CM − CAC is the after-ad figure. Monthly Rev = the whole month's DTC net revenue (every order booked that month, new and returning customers) — not cohort-lifetime revenue; it's the acquisition-month context for ad spend (Ad Spend ÷ Monthly Rev = that month's TACOS). Ad Spend = total Meta + Google spend in the acquisition month (the CAC numerator); CAC = Ad Spend ÷ new customers. Watch this for context: in early 2023 paid spend was minimal (Meta didn't start until Feb 2023), so those months show a very low CAC and an unusually high LTV:CAC that doesn't compare to later, fully-paid months. Use the Profit basis toggle to switch the CM/Cust, Total and LTV:CAC columns between Contribution Margin (after shipping + card processing) and Gross Profit (revenue − COGS only, before shipping/processing) — Gross runs higher since it bears fewer costs. LTV:CAC = profit per customer ÷ that month's CAC (≥3x 1–3x <1x). Click a cohort month to open its MTD page. Use the toggle to split by first-order type: Blended = all new customers, One-time = first order was not a subscription, Subscription = first order was a subscription. New Cust, repeat rate, LTV and totals are segment-specific; Ad Spend and CAC stay cohort-level (paid spend can't be cleanly split by first-order type), so a segment's LTV:CAC reads its own CM/cust against the blended acquisition cost. Sub-First % = share of that cohort whose first order was a subscription (purple = subscription, track = one-time); it's a cohort property, so it stays the same across the segment toggle. Caveats: guest checkouts (no customer ID) excluded; shipping cost is the actual ShipStation cost mapped per order (pre-2024 orders with no match use a $$7.50 fallback); CAC spreads the acquisition month's ad spend across that month's new customers (cohort-level).

📊 Projected 2-Year LTV — Recent Cohorts
How this works: For each cohort that hasn't reached 24 months yet, we take its actual cumulative LTV at its current age, divide by the % of 2-yr LTV historically captured by that age (the "velocity curve" from complete cohorts), and scale to month 24.
Example: a cohort at 12 months with $90 actual LTV — if complete cohorts captured 73% of their 2-yr value by month 12 — projects to $90 / 0.73 = $123.
Caveats: assumes recent cohorts will retain like historical ones. If product mix or subscriber rate has shifted materially, projections drift. Cohorts younger than 6 months are flagged "very low confidence" — small denominator, high variance.

💰 Contribution Margin LTV vs CAC — Are These Cohorts Actually Profitable?
The real profitability question. Gross LTV (revenue per customer) is just an input; what matters is contribution margin per customer compared to cost to acquire them.

Contribution Margin LTV = Net LTV − COGS − Shipping cost — the same lean definition as the Monthly KPI dashboard (ad spend shown separately as CAC, below).
  • COGS: SUM(line items × cogs_per_unit) per order — ~99% revenue coverage from the COGS table
  • Shipping: actual ShipStation cost per order when matched (2024+); $7.50 fallback for pre-2024 orders
  • Excluded (to match the KPI): card processing and the shipping-revenue credit. CM − CAC = the after-ad figure.

CAC = (Meta + Google ad spend in cohort acquisition month) ÷ (# new DTC customers acquired in that month). Treats all paid spend as acquisition cost — slightly overstates CAC since some spend goes to retargeting.

Profitable cohort: CM-LTV / CAC ≥ 1.0x means the cohort's 2-year contribution margin covers what we paid to acquire them. Industry rule of thumb: ≥3x is healthy.
What's NOT modeled: returns/refunds, organic costs (Klaviyo/Attentive subscriptions), warehousing, support, taxes. So actual cohort profit is somewhat lower than CM-LTV.
Reconciliation with the cohort grid above: both use the same cohorts (true first-order customers, Shopify lifetime order #1), so customer counts match exactly. Any small per-cohort gap is the shipping-cost estimate only — this section uses per-order matched shipping (with a fallback), while the grid uses a flat carrier-average per order.

🔁 Windowed Repurchase Rate — % of cohort placing a 2nd order within N days
Repurchase rate at day N = % of customers in a cohort who placed a 2nd order within N days of their 1st order. Subscription auto-ships count just like manual reorders.
This is the conventional ecommerce operational repurchase metric — complements the lifetime "Repeat rate" in the headline (which is ≥2 orders within 24 months).
Cohort eligibility: a cohort contributes to a given window only if that window has fully elapsed since cohort end-of-month. So the 30-day blend uses every cohort since 2023-01, but the 730-day blend uses only complete cohorts.
