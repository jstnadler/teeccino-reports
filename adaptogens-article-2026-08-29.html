The closing paragraph says the Miraculous Mushrooms collection has five adaptogenic blends. It has six — Cordyceps Schisandra Cinnamon Berry is missing from the list. It is an active product in both tea and coffee.

We have not changed your wording. The fix would be two words and one product name: “six adaptogenic blends” and “Five of them combine a single adaptogenic herb with a mushroom such as Chaga Ashwagandha, Reishi Eleuthero, Lion’s Mane Rhodiola, Turkey Tail Astragalus and Cordyceps Schisandra”. Say the word and it is done.
