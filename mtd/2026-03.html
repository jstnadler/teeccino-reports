How to read this. Each cohort is the set of customers whose first-ever Teeccino order landed in that month (DTC only; wholesale excluded). Once acquired, a customer stays in their cohort forever — these figures are cumulative through 2026-06-09 and refresh nightly, so an older month keeps growing as those customers reorder. Repeat Rate = share with 2+ lifetime orders (subscription rebills count). True Gross = MSRP × paid units. Net Revenue = cash collected (total_price, the pipeline's net convention). Contribution Margin = Net − sales tax − COGS − estimated shipping cost; it excludes ad spend, which is shown separately as acquisition CAC. LTV:CAC = CM per customer ÷ that month's CAC (≥3x 1–3x <1x). Caveats: guest checkouts (no customer ID) are excluded; shipping cost is allocated at the carrier average of $7.66/order because ShipStation rows don't join cleanly to Shopify orders; CAC allocates the acquisition month's total ad spend across that month's new customers (cohort-level, not per-order).
