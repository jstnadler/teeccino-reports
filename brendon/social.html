    How to read this

    • Instagram only. TikTok, Pinterest, Facebook, Stories and comment text aren't in this data.
    • Account reach is much larger than post reach. Account reach counts anyone who saw anything from us that day; post reach counts that post only. We haven't verified what makes up the difference, so don't add them up.
    • Engagement rate here is interactions divided by reach. Post-level uses that post's reach; account-level uses the day's account reach.
    • Daily account data starts . Earlier rows only have follower counts. The latest day is left out because Instagram's numbers for it are incomplete.
    • Post counts come from the post list. Instagram's own daily "posts published" figure can differ by a couple of posts.
    • Times are Pacific. Giveaway and theme tags are matched from caption wording; a post that only mentions an upcoming or past giveaway isn't tagged.
