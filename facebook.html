    How to read this

    • Facebook Page only. Instagram, TikTok and Pinterest are not in this view, and the Instagram dashboard counts things differently, so don't compare numbers across the two.
    • Engagements here means reactions + comments + shares on a post. It is not Facebook's own "post engagements" metric, which also counts clicks and isn't available to us yet.
    • Shares: Meta leaves the share count out when a post has none. We treat a missing share count as zero in these totals.
    • Counts are lifetime totals as of the latest read (). Older posts have had time to collect everything; the newest posts are still gathering reactions, so the most recent days will look low.
    • Followers: Meta only reports the current total, so our history starts the day we began saving it. Nothing is filled in for earlier dates.
    • Post data runs from . Posts before 2024 exist on the Page but aren't loaded here. Times are Pacific. Giveaway and theme tags are matched from caption wording.
