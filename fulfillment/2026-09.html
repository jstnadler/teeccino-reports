The warehouse ships on business days, so this window contains 2 weekend days and 1 weekday(s) with no shipments (holiday or shutdown). Per Shipping Day (161) divides by the 5 days that actually shipped and is the real throughput rate. Per Calendar Day (100) divides the same orders by all 8 days and so understates output by 38% — it is shown only so the two are not mistaken for each other. Shipment data runs through 2026-09-08.
