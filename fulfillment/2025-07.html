Fulfilled Orders per Day — July 2025
This panel is measured on ship date, not order date — and on a wider set of orders than the panels above it. Every other panel on this page answers “how fast did an order ship?” for DTC orders only, because that is where the service promise lives. This one answers “how much did the pick-and-pack team put out the door each day?”, so it counts DTC plus Target plus Walmart — those are picked and packed here alongside everything else, and leaving them out understated the team. Different question, different axis, different population: a figure here does not reconcile against a figure above, by design.

The warehouse ships on business days, so this window contains 8 weekend days and 1 weekday(s) with no shipments (holiday or shutdown). Per Shipping Day (135) divides by the 22 days that actually shipped and is the real throughput rate. Per Calendar Day (96) divides the same orders by all 31 days and so understates output by 29% — it is shown only so the two are not mistaken for each other. Shipment data runs through 2025-07-31.
Counts 2,972 orders — DTC, Target and Walmart — which is 96.3% of the 3,085 orders that shipped in this window. The other 113: 113 non-Shopify shipments (SO… and WW… order numbers from other systems, with no Shopify order to match). The non-Shopify shipments are real warehouse work this panel does not yet count; they are named rather than absorbed so the shortfall stays visible. An order is counted once, on its first shipment date.
