By surface

Click-to-order rate = Amazon-attributed orders ÷ GA4 clicks on that surface. The two systems count independently (GA4 counts the click on our site; Amazon counts the visit it received), so read it as a ratio of two sources, not a funnel step. Shown only once a surface has clicks.

Top products by clicks

Products are identified by their teeccino.com product page (Shopify handle). A product page can cover several sizes, so this is a page, not a specific item number. "Search" means the link sent the visitor to an Amazon.ca search rather than a single listing.
