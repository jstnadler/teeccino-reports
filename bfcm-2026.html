Where sales are coming from

Based on each order's last visit before buying, as recorded by Shopify (last click). Last year = same days of last year's sale, with today cut off at the same time of day. Email and text-message sales are recognised by their tracking tags; a Google Ads click that arrives without a tracking tag is counted as unpaid search. "Not yet known" = Shopify has not attached the visit history yet (usually fills in within a few hours). Subscription renewals are placed automatically and have no visit.
