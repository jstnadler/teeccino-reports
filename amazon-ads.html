Efficiency — ACOS vs TACOS
ACOS TACOS

Both are percentages, so they share one axis. ACOS = spend ÷ ad-attributed sales (how the ads themselves perform). TACOS = spend ÷ total Amazon sales (how much of the whole channel ad spend consumes) — the cross-table metric Amazon’s own console can’t show, since total sales come from SP-API. ROAS is deliberately not plotted: it is the exact reciprocal of ACOS.

From the spAdvertisedProduct report level — ad cost attributed to the specific SKU/ASIN advertised. Same-SKU is the share of that SKU’s ad sales which were actually the advertised product; the remainder is halo (the click sold something else), which is why per-SKU ACOS alone overstates how well a SKU converts itself. SKU identity here is Amazon’s seller SKU — join to CatalogSmith’s product master for margin, rather than matching on these strings directly (pack/channel suffixes such as L mean a naive join double-counts).
