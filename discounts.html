How this reconciles to the MTD dashboard

This page frames discount cost as Promotional Cost + Recurring Subscription (free gifts valued at COGS). The MTD dashboard's Total Discounts card uses dtc_discounts = True Gross − Subtotal — a clean order-level residual that is line-item only (no shipping, no free items) and can't double-count. Both are correct; they measure different scopes. Here's the bridge for May 2026:

Promotional Cost + Recurring Subscription (this page) $24,533
Less: Free Shipping promos (sit below Subtotal, not in Gross−Subtotal) −$232
Less: Free Gifts at COGS (items given away, not a price discount) −$2,956
Line-item discounts only (promotions + all subscription implied) $21,345
Less: category overlap (same dollars booked twice — e.g. a Stay.ai subscription discount also tagged as a Shopify automatic discount)−$74
MTD dashboard — Total Discounts (dtc_discounts) $21,271

Rule of thumb: this page is the true-cost view — it counts the free-shipping lever and values free gifts at what they actually cost us. The MTD card is the clean line-item residual. The gap between them is shipping, free gifts, and a small Stay.ai double-count.
