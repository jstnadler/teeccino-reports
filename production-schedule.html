How the schedule is derived demand to run size, in four steps

1. Demand0.4 × median(Sep 25–Mar 26) + 0.6 × median(Apr–Jul 26). Median, not mean, so promo spikes do not inflate the plan.
2. PoundsUnits × fill weight from the BOM. Pounds are the only unit common to sales and production.
3. Cycle4 weeks by default; stretched to 8/12/16 where a 4-week run would fall under the 150 lb minimum batch.
4. Run sizeCycle × weekly demand, split into 2,000 lb large-blender batches. Order-up-to is (4 + cycle) weeks.
