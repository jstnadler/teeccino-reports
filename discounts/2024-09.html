How this reconciles to the MTD dashboard

This page frames discount cost as Promotional Cost + Recurring Subscription (free gifts valued at COGS). The MTD dashboard's Total Discounts card uses dtc_discounts = True Gross − Subtotal — a clean order-level residual that is line-item only (no shipping, no free items) and can't double-count. Both are correct; they measure different scopes. Here's the bridge for September 2024:

Rule of thumb: this page is the true-cost view — it counts the free-shipping lever and values free gifts at what they actually cost us. The MTD card is the clean line-item residual. The gap between them is shipping, free gifts, and a small Stay.ai double-count.

What Each Category Means

Automatic Discounts
Shopify automatic discounts — FOTM auto-promos, sitewide sales, app-sourced discount functions (no code needed).
Source: SUM(dtc_automatic_discounts)
Discount Codes
Checkout discount codes — email/SMS promos, influencer codes, win-back codes.
Source: SUM(dtc_code_discounts)
Manual Discounts
Staff-applied manual discounts / adjustments (customer service, one-off corrections).
Source: SUM(dtc_manual_discounts)
Price Reduction Discounts
Items listed BELOW MSRP — temporary product-level price cuts / sales (e.g. Earth Day spring sale). Shopify doesn't tag these as discount allocations because the customer just sees the lower price; computed per line as MSRP − unit_price on non-subscription items.
Source: SUM(dtc_price_reductions)
Free Shipping Promo
Free-shipping waives — threshold-based free ship, free-ship promo codes, and app-sourced free-ship functions. The shipping lever.
Source: SUM(dtc_shipping_discounts)
Free Gifts (at COGS)
Items given away (BOGO, FOTM samples, GWP) valued at our actual cost (COGS), not retail — what free gifts truly cost us.
Source: SUM(dtc_free_item_cogs)
First-time Subscription
Implied discount on first-time subscriber orders — the welcome / acquisition offer. MSRP − unit_price on first-sub line items.
Source: raw: first-time subscription implied discount
Recurring Subscription (structural)
Implied discount on repeat subscriber orders — ongoing subscriber pricing, not a marketing promotion. Shown in the hero and the table's context column; deliberately excluded from Promotional Cost.
Source: raw: recurring subscription implied discount
