How this reconciles to the top KPI card

This page frames discount cost as Promotional Cost + Recurring Subscription (free gifts valued at COGS). The top-of-page DTC Discounts card uses dtc_discounts = True Gross − Subtotal — a clean order-level residual that is line-item only (no shipping, no free items) and can't double-count. Both are correct; they measure different scopes. Here's the bridge for YTD 2024:

Promotional Cost + Recurring Subscription (this page) $349,155
Less: Free Shipping promos (sit below Subtotal, not in Gross−Subtotal) −$2,006
Less: Free Gifts at COGS (items given away, not a price discount) −$32,172
Line-item discounts only (promotions + all subscription implied) $314,977
Plus: uncategorized residual (line-item discount in the subtotal gap not captured by a named bucket)+$3,092
Top-of-page — DTC Discounts (dtc_discounts) $318,068

Rule of thumb: this page is the true-cost view — it counts the free-shipping lever and values free gifts at what they actually cost us. The MTD card is the clean line-item residual. The gap between them is shipping, free gifts, and a small Stay.ai double-count.
